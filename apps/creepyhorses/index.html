the creepy horses

four oil paintings, hung in a quiet hallway by an aunt who never met a horse but had strong feelings about them. tap a painting. the horses do not like to be looked at.

↓ click any painting ↓

about the works

oil on canvas, late period of the artist, who is your aunt. the proportions are not wrong on purpose, but they are not wrong by accident either. somewhere in between.

fashion notes

each horse was painted in its sunday best: a scarf, a bonnet, a collar of bells, sunglasses (pre-existing). the hats were not painted. the hats are added later, when no one is looking.

the noises

nothing is sampled. every sound is a small synth made of square waves and regret. the horses' mouths are painted shut. the noise comes from somewhere else. probably the next room.

visiting

do not stand too close. do not turn your back on the third one. the gift shop is closed. the gift shop has always been closed.
