What should the AI layer do?

You chose titles + content + commands as searchable types. The AI layer sits below instant results. What should it surface?

A

Semantic Content Match

AI understands meaning, not just keywords. Searching "user onboarding" also finds paragraphs about "first-time experience" or "signup flow" even if those exact words aren't used. Returns document snippets ranked by semantic relevance.

Practical, high-value. Makes content search dramatically better without being flashy.

B

Question Answering

When the query looks like a question, AI reads across your documents and answers it directly with a synthesized response + source citations. "What's our pricing strategy?" returns a composed answer drawn from relevant docs, not just links.

The Notion Q&A / Mem AI pattern. Powerful but higher latency and token cost.

C

Both (Semantic + Q&A)

Short queries get semantic content matching. Queries that look like questions get a synthesized answer. The system auto-detects which mode to use.

Most powerful, but most complex to build. Could start with semantic and add Q&A later.
